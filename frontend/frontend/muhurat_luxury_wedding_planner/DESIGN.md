## Brand & Style

This design system blends regal Rajasthani heritage—evocative of the grand havelis and palaces of Jaipur and Udaipur—with high-precision modern enterprise SaaS design. The aesthetic bridges old-world Indian courtly elegance with executive operational efficiency.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Architectural & Majestic:** Drawing inspiration from jharokhas, carved stone screens, and sandstone courtyards through balanced symmetry, crisp warm structural lines, and dignified palettes.
- **Calm Mastery & Reassurance:** High-stakes royal weddings require operational poise. The UI instills total confidence for planners handling multi-crore budgets, celebrity logistics, and sacred muhurat timelines.
- **Opulent Yet Grounded:** Extravagance is signaled through restrained jewel-tone pigments and metallic highlights rather than heavy skeuomorphism or busy decorative motifs.

### Design Movement
**Regal Minimalist SaaS:** Clean card-driven enterprise utility anchored by a deep maroon architectural shell, ivory canvas workspaces, delicate warm-beige framing, and subtle brushed-gold accents. Data density remains high and scannable without ever feeling clinical or industrial.

## Colors

The palette derives from royal Mewar and Rajputana ceremonial textiles, aged gold vellum, and dry marble courtyards, calibrated for WCAG AAA accessibility across text and key interactive triggers.

### Core Tonal System
- **Deep Maroon (`#7B1E3A`):** The primary brand anchor. Used for primary interactive buttons, primary headers, badges of honor, and the fixed architectural left navigation shell.
- **Antique Gold (`#C9A24B`):** The secondary metallic accent. Utilized for active navigation indicators, focus rings, key milestone highlights, badge trims, and decorative framing accents.
- **Warm Ivory (`#FBF6EC`):** The foundational canvas layer. Warmer than stark device whites, evoking handmade parchment and fine hospitality stationery.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** The primary surface container for elevated cards, modal drawers, and operational data tables.
- **Warm Beige Border (`#EADFC9`):** Structural perimeter tone. Provides crisp definition without the abrasive contrast of standard grey wireframing.
- **Primary Charcoal (`#2B2B2B`):** Deep soot tone ensuring crisp text rendering across high-density data tables and body copy.
- **Muted Sandstone (`#6B655B`):** Secondary metadata, table headers, breadcrumbs, and timestamp indicators.

### Status Tones (Celebration & Operations)
- **Success / Confirmed / Paid (`#2E7D5B`):** Deep forest emerald for completed vendor payouts, confirmed muhurat rituals, and RSVP acceptances.
- **Warning / Pending (`#E0A100`):** Rich amber saffron for contract negotiations, pending deposits, and schedule overlaps.
- **Danger / Overdue / Late (`#C62828`):** Royal vermilion for critical timeline delays, payment defaults, and vendor cancellations.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional tension between editorial prestige and enterprise precision:

- **Playfair Display:** Reserved strictly for display heroes, section titles, event names, royal palace headers, and high-level KPI values. It brings an editorial, ceremonial voice that honors wedding tradition.
- **Inter:** The functional workhorse powering all data tables, guest lists, Rupee calculations, input fields, navigation labels, and interactive states. Set with strict tracking and generous line heights to ensure fatigue-free reading during intense multi-day itinerary coordination.
- **Currency & Metric Formatting:** The Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) is rendered alongside `Inter` tab numbers to ensure vertical alignment throughout financial settlement ledgers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural grid designed to handle both panoramic wedding itinerary flows and compact budget ledgers.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Shell Structure:** A persistent, fixed-width left navigation rail (`260px`) framed in deep maroon, anchored beside a flexible canvas area filled with warm ivory (`#FBF6EC`).
- **Top Utility Bar:** A sticky `64px` height band on an ivory/white base, handling operational context (event venue, date countdown, current planner profile, quick actions).
- **Desktop Grid:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) canvas margins.
- **Mobile Reflow:** Below `768px`, the fixed sidebar collapses into an off-canvas drawer accessed via a gold-trimmed burger toggle; the canvas grid condenses to 4 columns with `1rem` margins.
- **Rhythm:** Multi-card operational views (e.g., guest check-in rosters, baraat milestones) align to strict 8pt component intervals, avoiding erratic card heights.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain a crisp, aristocratic aesthetic, this design system eschews muddy, heavy drop-shadows in favor of low-contrast delicate outlines and warm, luminous tonal transitions.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Layer (`#FBF6EC`):** Deepest structural base for all content canvases and backdrop spaces.
2. **Surface Tier (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white cards, side drawers, and tables. All cards are outlined with a continuous `1px solid #EADFC9` border.
3. **Elevated Overlays & Modals (`#FFFFFF`):** Floating dialogs, settlement approvals, and guest profile drawers receive an ultra-soft amber-tinted shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(123, 30, 58, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.04)`, retaining crisp `1px solid #EADFC9` edging.
4. **Shell Navigation Tier (`#7B1E3A`):** The fixed sidebar sits at elevation index 0 flat against the screen, commanding depth entirely through rich color contrast.

## Shapes

The design system employs a tailored, consistent `10px` curvature across structural cards, panels, inputs, and modals, balancing contemporary ergonomics with the architectural geometry of palace stonework.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Cards, Panels & Data Tables:** Uniformly `10px` radius, cleanly encasing content without harsh corners or overly rounded informal shapes.
- **Inputs & Action Buttons:** `8px` to `10px`, providing a solid tactile feel for operational tasks.
- **Pills & Status Badges:** Fully circular (`9999px`) rounded caps for event tags, staff roles, and payment status labels.
- **Avatar & Emblem Containers:** Circular (`9999px`) for portraits and Royal Knot emblems, or slightly softened square (`8px`) for venue architectural vignettes.

## Components

### 1. Navigation Shell & Sidebar
- **Background:** Solid deep maroon (`#7B1E3A`).
- **Brand Emblem:** Subtle antique gold (`#C9A24B`) Royal Knot insignia paired with Playfair Display brand mark.
- **Navigation Items:** 
  - Standard state: Off-white/cream text (`#FBF6EC`) with 75% opacity, sans-serif 14px medium weight.
  - Active state: Opaque antique gold (`#C9A24B`) text, warm gold background tint (`rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.12)`), and an unambiguous left border (`3px solid #C9A24B`) spanning the full height of the item.
  - Core link items: *Enquiries, Agreements, Quotes, Wedding Plan, Muhurat Timeline, Settlement, Season Review*.

### 2. Header & Context Top Bar
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` or translucent warm ivory with delicate bottom border (`1px solid #EADFC9`).
- **Event Metadata:** Highlighting current couple and palace venue (e.g., *Rathore & Shekhawat Wedding | Rambagh Palace, Jaipur*).
- **Profile Module:** Staff portrait or monogram circle, user name (e.g., *Pooja Rathore*), accompanied by a role pill badge (*Founder*, *Senior Planner*, or *Floor Staff*).
- **Action Group:** Gold-accented quick-actions (+ New Vendor PO, + Guest Entry) and a minimalist, muted *Sign Out* action button.

### 3. Buttons
- **Primary:** Background deep maroon (`#7B1E3A`), pure white text, `8px` to `10px` border-radius. On hover: Darkened maroon (`#63152C`) with a crisp `0 2px 8px rgba(123, 30, 58, 0.2)` subtle lift.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Transparent ivory fill with an antique gold border (`1px solid #C9A24B`), text colored in maroon or antique gold. On hover: `rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.08)` wash.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, muted charcoal text, turning maroon on hover.

### 4. Cards & Information Panels
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface container, `10px` border radius, bordered with `1px solid #EADFC9`.
- **Card Headers:** Playfair Display for card titles, accompanied by an optional right-aligned gold timestamp or status pill.

### 5. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Base Style:** Pure white background, `1px solid #EADFC9` border, `8px` radius, `14px` Inter typography.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to antique gold (`#C9A24B`) with a soft glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.2)`).
- **Currency Inputs:** Permanent prepended `₹` prefix locked in muted stone grey (`#6B655B`).

### 6. Chips & Status Badges
- **Shape:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with `space-xs` vertical padding and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- **Paid / Confirmed / Arrived:** Light emerald tint background (`#EAF5F0`), deep forest green text (`#2E7D5B`), delicate green border.
- **Pending / Scheduled:** Soft warm amber tint background (`#FEF8E7`), rich amber saffron text (`#E0A100`).
- **Overdue / Alert:** Light rose tint background (`#FDECEE`), vermilion text (`#C62828`).
- **Role Pills (Top Bar):** Ivory background, antique gold border (`1px solid #C9A24B`), charcoal text.

### 7. Tables & Muhurat Timeline Lists
- **Header Row:** Warm ivory (`#FBF6EC`) background, muted charcoal labels (`12px` uppercase, letter-spacing `0.05em`), clean horizontal border (`1px solid #EADFC9`).
- **Data Rows:** Zebra-free pure white surfaces with horizontal dividers (`1px solid #F3ECE0`), highlighting on row hover with ivory glow.
- **Muhurat Timeline Component:** Chronological vertical milestone rail linked via an antique gold vertical guide wire. Auspicious timings are flagged with a miniature gold sun icon and bolded charcoal time stamps.
